# Backtest: breakout scanner V1, 2025-04-30 to 2026-11-03

> Simulated research result on historical data. Not a recommendation, not a forecast, and
> not evidence that the strategy will make money. Past simulated returns do not guarantee
> future returns. No orders were placed.

> **DATA QUALITY WARNING:** 7 held-position gaps were simulated at the previous close,
> potentially a non-executable price. Results may overstate performance. This is an
> exploratory baseline, **not a validated or deployable strategy**.

## Data and method

- **Data:** official NSE bhavcopy files, mainboard `EQ` series, every stock that traded on each day, including ones later delisted (no survivorship filter). Prices are **not** split/bonus adjusted; a stock's history restarts at any overnight gap over 25% or missing session.
- **Period:** 375 trading sessions after a 200-session warm-up. One market regime; too short for a real walk-forward test. Parameters were fixed in advance, not fitted to this data.
- **Signals:** `strategy.scan_breakout` with default `BreakoutConfig` (20-session high, volume ≥1.5×, 63-session return ≥30%, above 50/200-session averages, ₹3 crore average turnover).
- **Entry:** next session's open + 0.25% slippage; skipped if that open is more than 3% above the signal close, at or below the stop, or locked at a circuit. Strongest 63-session momentum first.
- **Size:** `risk.size_position`: planned loss at the stop ≤0.5% of equity, position ≤10% of equity, limited by cash.
- **Exits:** stop 7% below the signal close (filled at the stop, or at the open if the price gapped through it, minus slippage); sell at the next open after a close below the 20-session average. No fill on a day locked at a circuit.
- **Charges (Upstox delivery, 2026-11-04):** ₹20 per order, STT 0.1% both sides, NSE 0.00307%, SEBI ₹10/crore, stamp 0.015% on buys, DP ₹20 per sale, GST 18% on fees.
- **Cash:** ₹100,000 at the start, ₹20,000 on the first session of each month. Existing Groww holdings are not modelled.
- **Benchmark:** Nifty 500 price index (Upstox), bought at the close with the same deposits. No dividends and no fund costs, so it is understated by roughly the dividend yield.
